How are these joints numbered? {% if model.kind == "robot" %}

Numbers 1–{{ model.dof }} are this archive’s display labels. We group independently movable URDF joints by body region and sort their source names within each group. Fixed and mimic joints do not receive separate controls. The URDF author supplies the names, axes, limits and hierarchy.

These labels are not manufacturer motor IDs, URDF file order or a simulator’s joint-vector order. Identify a joint by its source name and use the target SDK or controller’s documented mapping when commanding hardware.

{% elsif model.kind == "character" %}

This character’s 16 joint indices are authored for the website: 0 root, 1 chest, 2 neck, 3 head, 4–6 left arm, 7–9 right arm, 10–11 feet, 12 jaw, 13–14 eyes and 15 nose. Each joint has three local rotation controls.

These indices are not SMPL indices, official Disney rig identifiers or robot motor IDs. The export includes the complete names, parent hierarchy and authored rest positions.

{% else %}

Numbers start at 0 and follow the model’s pose-parameter joint order. They are distinct from mesh-vertex indices, detected landmarks and additional joints produced by a joint regressor. One human pose joint normally has three rotation parameters.

SMPL and STAR use 0–23. SMPL+H uses body joints 0–21, left fingers 22–36 and right fingers 37–51. SMPL-X inserts jaw and eyes at 22–24, then uses left fingers 25–39 and right fingers 40–54. Hand chains follow index, middle, pinky, ring, thumb order.

{% if model.id == "smpl-h" %}

Sharpa mode is a hybrid: 22 body joints plus 22 robot joints per hand placement. Its L/R display labels and URDF joint commands are exported separately; they are not native SMPL+H hand parameters.
